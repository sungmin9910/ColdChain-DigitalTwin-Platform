The  agricultural  product  supply  chain,  spanning  from  farms  to 
lack  of 
consumers, 
transparency  across  these  stages  often  results  in  quality  degradation, 
inefficient  inventory  management,  and  diminished  consumer  trust. 
Traditional  static  QR  codes  store  only  fixed  information  captured  at  the 
time  of  production,  making  it  difficult  to  reflect  new  data  generated 
during  later  stages  of  the  supply  chain.  To  address  this  limitation, 
digitalization  of  supply  chain  management  requires  systems  capable  of 
real-time  data  updating  and  sharing.    Dynamic  QR  codes,  connected  to 
databases through redirect URLs, enable continuous information updates 
and  transparent  tracking  of  production,  processing,  and  logistics 
throughout  the  entire  product  lifecycle.  In  this  study,  a  real-time  data 
management  and  traceability  system  based  on  dynamic  QR  codes  was 
designed  and  implemented  to  cover  the  entire  workflow  from  farms  to 
consumers.  QR  codes  generated  at  each  stage  were  automatically 
synchronized with a centralized database, while a graphical user interface 
(GUI)  allowed  workers  to  intuitively  enter  and  access  information. 
Integrated  QR  code  scanners  facilitated  automatic  collection  of  key  data 
such  as  location,  environmental  conditions,  grading  results,  and 
processing times. As a result, all process information—from harvesting to 
consumption—was  recorded  in  real  time  and  made  instantly  accessible 
via QR codes. System application demonstrated practical benefits: farmers 
could  review  processing  and  distribution  data  to  inform  cultivation  and 
management  practices;  workers  could  efficiently  check  production  and 
storage  information  to  optimize  inventory  and  logistics  management  at 
agricultural processing centers (APCs); and consumers could scan product 
QR codes to verify production history, cultivation methods, quality grades, 
and distribution channels, thereby strengthening trust in food safety and 
product  quality.  This  study  highlights  that  dynamic  QR  codes  serve  not 
only as tools for delivering information but also as a digital backbone for 
modern  agricultural  supply  chains.  In  particular,  their  ability  to  support 
real-time  data  updates  and  information  sharing  enhances  traceability, 
streamlines inventory management, and promotes transparency across all 
stakeholders.

Conclusions

This  study  has  investigated  a  real-time  data  management  and 
traceability  system  using  dynamic  QR  codes,  focusing  on  the 
process  from  the  moment  agricultural  products  are  received  at 
the  Agricultural  Processing  Center  (APC)  until  they  reach  the 
consumer.  All  information  generated  at  each  stage  within  the 
APC—including  receiving,  washing,  sorting,  packaging,  and 
shipping—is  recorded  and  synchronized  in  real  time  onto  the 
dynamic  QR  code.  Through  this  system,  the  APC  can  maximize 
the efficiency of its internal processes and perform precise, data-
logistics  management.  Furthermore,  consumers  can 
driven 
enhance  their  trust  in  product  quality  by  directly  verifying  the 
transparent  processing  history  from  the  APC  simply  by  scanning 
the QR code. In conclusion, dynamic QR codes have shown high 
potential  as  a  key  technology  for  enhancing  supply  chain 
transparency  and  efficiency  by  digitally  integrating  the  complex 
internal processes of the APC and the distribution chain.
